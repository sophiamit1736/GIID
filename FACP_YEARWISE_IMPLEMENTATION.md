# FACP recording model in LearnAble

The supplied FACP document says: Entry assessment on admission, then periodic evaluation at every quarter (once in 3 months), with space for recording progress over about three academic years in a given level.

LearnAble currently records `Entry` and `Quarter 1`–`Quarter 4`. Academic year and year-in-level fields remain in the database only for backward compatibility and are not used by the current UI or scoring. GIID local year-wise recording/promotion rules should be confirmed with the project officer before automating a year calculation.

Quantitative score: + and C are achievement observations; NA is removed from the total; GP/VP/M/PP/-/NE are recorded but are not achievement points. Recreational A/B/C/D/E/NE is separate. 80% is the source promotion criterion.
